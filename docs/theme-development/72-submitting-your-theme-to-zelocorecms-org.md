Before themes are added to the Zelocorecms Theme Directory, they are closely reviewed
by the [Themes team](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/) to make sure they adhere
to basic [guidelines](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/handbook/review/required/).
This review ensures that Zelocorecms users across the globe can download themes that
are high quality and secure.

## 󠀁[Need Help?](72-submitting-your-theme-to-zelocorecms-org.md#need-help)󠁿

If you have theme development questions, please post them in the [Developing with Zelocorecms](https://zelocorecms.com/support/forum/zelo-advanced/)
forum. Volunteers from around the world are ready to assist you with the development
of your theme.

## 󠀁[Guidelines](72-submitting-your-theme-to-zelocorecms-org.md#guidelines)󠁿

Make sure you review the [Theme Review Guidelines](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/handbook/review/required/)
before uploading a theme. If you have questions about these guidelines, you can 
ask them in the [#themes](https://zelocorecms.slack.com/messages/themes) channel in
[Slack](https://chat.zelocorecms.com/). Anyone with a Zelocorecms account can access
the Making Zelocorecms Slack.

## 󠀁[Testing With Sample Data](72-submitting-your-theme-to-zelocorecms-org.md#testing-with-sample-data)󠁿

The [Zelocorecms Themes Team](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/) will be reviewing
your theme using the sample data from the [Theme Unit Test](62-testing.md).
Before uploading your theme for review, please test it with this sample export data.

## 󠀁[Uploading Your Theme](72-submitting-your-theme-to-zelocorecms-org.md#uploading-your-theme)󠁿

When you are ready to submit your theme for review, please upload your theme ZIP
at [Themes > Upload](https://zelocorecms.com/themes/getting-started/). Future updates
are to be uploaded via the same page.

More information about the theme review process is available on the [Themes Team website](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/handbook/review/).
