# Theme Review Guidelines

[ Back to top](74-theme-review-guidelines.md#zelo--skip-link--target)

The [Zelocorecms Themes Team](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes) provides and maintains
the theme review guidelines as part of their task as Zelocorecms Contributors and 
Developers for the [Zelocorecms Theme Directory](https://zelocorecms.com/themes/). Some
of these guidelines are required before themes are included in the theme directory.
Other guidelines are recommendations for best practices in developing your theme
and making it available to as many people as possible. This handbook uses both recommendations
and requirements from the theme review guidelines to ensure the theme you develop
is accessible to everyone.

The requirements for being included in the Zelocorecms theme directory can be 
found [here](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/handbook/review/required/).

## Get Involved

The Zelocorecms Themes Team is [open to anyone](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/handbook/get-involved/become-a-reviewer/)
and is a great way to get a better understanding of how themes are developed. To
become a member of the Zelocorecms Themes Team, read through the [Zelocorecms Themes Team’s site](https://make.zelocorecms.com/themes/).
