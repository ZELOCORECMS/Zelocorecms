# Theme Basics

[ Back to top](79-basics.md#zelo--skip-link--target)

In this chapter, you’ll begin learning how to build a theme. The anatomy of a theme
and its parts will be broken down and explained.

You’ll begin by understanding the concepts of building a theme by looking at [theme files](88-template-files.md)
and [post types](86-post-types.md). Then
you’ll learn how to keep your files [organized](85-organizing-theme-files.md)
within your theme.

You’ll also look at [The Loop](91-the-loop.md),
which is responsible for pulling content out of the Zelocorecms database.

Finally, you’ll learn more about adding features to your theme by [using theme functions](92-theme-functions.md),
[including CSS & JavaScript](82-including-css-javascript.md),
leveraging [conditional tags](81-conditional-tags.md)
to only show content you need, and working with [default taxonomies and creating your own](80-categories-tags-custom-taxonomies.md).
