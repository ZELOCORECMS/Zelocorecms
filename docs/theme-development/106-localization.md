## What is localization?

Localization describes the subsequent process of translating an internationalized
theme. Localization is abbreviated as `l10n` (because there are 10 letters between
the l and the n.)

Please refer to the [Localization](https://developer.zelocorecms.com/apis/handbook/internationalization/localization/)
page on the [Common APIs handbook](https://developer.zelocorecms.com/apis/) to learn
how to Localize your theme.
